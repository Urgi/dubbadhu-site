About Dubbadhu

Afaan Oromo language learning app · Afaan LLC · Last updated September 10, 2026

Dubbadhu means “speak” in Afaan Oromo. The brand promise is Speak with Confidence: native conversation and speaking practice, not flashcards. It is the Afaan Oromo language learning app from Afaan LLC—available on the App Store and Google Play. Follow product updates on Instagram @dubbadhu.app and TikTok @dubbadhu.app. Website: dubbadhu.com.

Who Dubbadhu is for

Dubbadhu is built for learners in Oromia and the Horn—and for US and diaspora families reconnecting with or retaining Afaan Oromo. Parents, children, and adults practice real conversation so they can speak with relatives, not memorize a deck of cards.

What Dubbadhu offers (Premium)

Full access to every speaking conversation—current and new releases.

A portion of each plan supports East Africa communities.

  • All current and new speaking conversations, validated by professional language teachers in Oromia
  • Conversation videos from native speakers
  • Unlimited AI practice and translate feedback
  • Vocabulary audio, quizzes, and lesson discussions

Why Dubbadhu is unique

  • Speaking-first, not flashcards — native conversation and speaking practice. Not flashcard drills.
  • Teacher-validated in Oromia — speaking content reviewed by professional language teachers.
  • Native-speaker conversation video — learn from real conversations, not text-only drills.
  • Unlimited AI speaking practice — Premium unlocks unlimited practice and translate feedback.
  • Built for the Horn and the diaspora — starts with Afaan Oromo for learners in Oromia and US/diaspora families; Amharic and Tigrinya are coming next.
  • Mission-backed — a portion of each subscription supports East Africa communities.
