Delete your Dubbadhu account

Last Updated: July 29, 2026

You can request deletion of your Dubbadhu account and associated personal data without reinstalling the app. Use the steps below — we process requests by email.

How to request deletion

  1. Email support@afaantech.com with the subject line Delete my Dubbadhu account.
  2. Include the phone number associated with your Dubbadhu account (the number you use to sign in).
  3. Optionally include your name and a brief note that you want your account and personal data deleted.

You can also start a request from this link: Email a deletion request.

What we delete

When we process your request, we delete or anonymize personal data associated with your account, including:

  • Account and profile information (phone number, name, email if provided)
  • In-app progress and usage data tied to your account
  • Lesson discussion posts associated with your account (where applicable)
  • Audio or other content you submitted, except where a separate legal retention requirement applies

What we may retain

We may retain limited information when required by law or for legitimate business needs, for example:

  • Transaction or subscription records needed for accounting, fraud prevention, or legal compliance
  • Records of the deletion request itself

App Store or Google Play subscriptions are billed by Apple or Google. Deleting your Dubbadhu account does not cancel a store subscription. Cancel auto-renew in App Store subscriptions or your Google Play subscription settings.

Timeline

After we verify your request, we will delete or anonymize your personal data within 30 days, consistent with our Privacy Policy.

In-app option

If you still have the app installed, you may also be able to delete your account from Profile or account settings. You can always use this page and email us instead.
