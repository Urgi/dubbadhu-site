Dubbadhu language roadmap

Horn & East Africa · SPEAK · LEARN · PRESERVE

Dubbadhu is building a speaking-first language learning platform for the Horn and East Africa—and for diaspora families retaining those languages. Afaan Oromo is on iOS and Android. Amharic and Tigrinya are in development.

Available now

  • Afaan Oromo — speaking-first curriculum (native conversation, not flashcards) with native-speaker video, AI practice, vocabulary audio, quizzes, and lesson discussions. Download on the App Store and Google Play. Details: Learn Afaan Oromo.

In development

  • Amharic — in development for the Dubbadhu platform
  • Tigrinya — in development for the Dubbadhu platform

Follow @dubbadhu.app on Instagram. Afaan Oromo is live now; join the Amharic & Tigrinya notify list for those launch updates.

Planned

Additional Horn and East African languages after Amharic and Tigrinya, with the same focus on speaking, native video, and culturally grounded curriculum.

What every Dubbadhu language course aims to include

  • Speaking conversations validated with language professionals
  • Conversation videos from native speakers
  • AI practice and translate feedback
  • Vocabulary audio, quizzes, and lesson discussions
