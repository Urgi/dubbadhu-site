Community Guidelines

Last Updated: May 27, 2026

Dubbadhu is built to help learners practice Afaan Oromo with confidence, respect, and encouragement.

Dubbadhu is operated by Afaan LLC. Reach our team at support@afaantech.com.

Lesson discussion posts are optional. When responding to lesson prompts in the app, you may choose to post with your name, post anonymously, or keep your response private and not post at all.

The purpose of lesson discussions is to help learners practice what they are learning, see examples from others, and build confidence using Afaan Oromo in a respectful community. Posts are tied to language-learning prompts within a lesson series — not a general social network.

Please follow these guidelines

Be respectful and encouraging

Language learning involves mistakes. Do not mock, shame, bully, or target others for how they write, speak, or learn. Celebrate effort and help others feel safe to practice.

Keep posts related to learning

Posts should be connected to the lesson prompt, Afaan Oromo practice, culture, encouragement, or the learning experience.

Do not post harmful content

Do not post harassment, hate speech, threats, sexually explicit content, spam, illegal content, or content that attacks people based on identity, background, religion, ethnicity, nationality, gender, or other personal characteristics.

Protect privacy

Do not share private information about yourself or others, including phone numbers, addresses, financial information, private conversations, or anything you do not have permission to share.

Use anonymous posting responsibly

Anonymous posting is available to help learners feel comfortable practicing. It should not be used to insult, harass, threaten, or avoid accountability for harmful behavior.

Moderation

Dubbadhu may remove posts that violate these guidelines or harm the learning environment. Repeated or serious violations may limit a user’s ability to participate in discussions.

Reporting and blocking

If you see a post that violates these guidelines, tap ⋯ on the post and choose Report post. You can also tap Block to stop seeing that learner’s posts in lesson discussions.

For other safety concerns about lesson discussions, email support@afaantech.com.

For account, subscription, or technical help, visit our Support page.
